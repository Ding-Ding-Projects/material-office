# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on behavior and code, never people.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, sexualized language, trolling, insulting comments, public or private harassment, and publishing another person’s private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers will investigate fairly, protect reporter privacy, and take proportionate corrective action. This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
